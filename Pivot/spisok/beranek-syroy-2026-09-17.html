Meet Beranek Realty Group

Meet Beranek Realty Group

Yana Beranek is the CEO of Beranek Realty Group and co-founder of International Real Estate Concierge, where she represents luxury buyers, sellers, and investors across Los Angeles and abroad. Licensed since 2013, Yana ranks among the top 0.5% of agents at Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices California Properties and is a multiple-time Chairman’s Circle Diamond Award winner. With over a decade of experience, she has become a trusted advisor in some of LA’s most sought-after markets, including Santa Monica, Pacific Palisades, Venice, Marina del Rey, and Beverly Hills. Known for her strategic, data-driven approach and ability to negotiate powerfully on behalf of her clients, Yana consistently delivers exceptional results in high-value transactions.

Her reach extends beyond Los Angeles. As Berkshire Hathaway’s Global Ambassador, Yana connects U.S.-based clients with investment opportunities worldwide, representing premier listings in Mexico, Portugal, Dubai, Cyprus, and beyond. She works closely with developers and international partners, providing concierge-level service for clients seeking global diversification. While her primary focus is on luxury and investment sales, she also advises on commercial properties and leases, ensuring clients receive comprehensive guidance across all aspects of real estate.

Fluent in both Russian and English, Yana is uniquely positioned to serve an international clientele with seamless communication and cultural understanding. Backed by more than 90 verified client reviews, her reputation is built on trust, integrity, and results. She combines local expertise with global reach, making her the go-to resource for high-net-worth individuals, families, and international investors.
