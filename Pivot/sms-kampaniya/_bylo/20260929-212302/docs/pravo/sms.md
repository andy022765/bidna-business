## Messaging Terms (English)

These terms govern SMS text messages sent by **Wealthboosterpro LLC** (DBA Business
Intelligence DNA), a Wyoming limited liability company, 5830 E 2nd St Ste 7000,
Casper, WY 82609, USA.

### 1. Program description

We operate **automated transactional messaging**. When you call our business line and
verbally ask us to send you a link to our materials, our system sends you a single
text message containing that link. We do not run marketing, promotional, or
recurring campaigns.

### 2. How you opt in

Consent is given **verbally, by you, during a call that you placed to us**. Our virtual
assistant asks for your mobile number and confirms that you want the link sent by text.
The request is recorded in the text transcript of that call, which we retain as evidence
of your consent.

We do not obtain numbers from purchased lists, third parties, or web scraping. We never
message a number that was not given to us by its owner during a call to us.

### 3. Message frequency

**You may receive up to 4 messages per month**, and only in direct response to your own
request. One request results in one message.

### 4. Cost

We do not charge for messages. **Message and data rates may apply** according to your
mobile carrier plan.

### 5. How to stop

Reply **STOP** to any message to stop receiving messages from us. You will receive one
final confirmation and no further messages. You may also reply **СТОП** (Russian).

Reply **HELP** or **ПОМОЩЬ** for assistance, or contact us using the details on
businessinteldna.com/contacts.

### 6. Privacy

**No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing
or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator
opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.**

Our messages are delivered through Twilio, a licensed communications provider acting
as our technical carrier, not as a recipient or purchaser of your data. Our full privacy
policy is at businessinteldna.com/privacy.

### 7. Carrier liability

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

### 8. Supported carriers

Messages are supported on all major US carriers. Delivery is not guaranteed on all
devices, on landline numbers, or on VoIP numbers.
