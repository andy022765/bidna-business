What CareLine does

CareLine is an AI voice assistant on your agency's phone lines. It follows the requirements and rules you set, and passes decisions to your staff.

Hiring line, day and night

Applicants call the number in your job ads at any hour. CareLine asks your screening questions, books the ones who meet your minimum into your interview calendar, and sends a confirmation and reminders. Your team decides who gets hired.

Shift call-offs

A caregiver calls or texts that she can't make a shift. CareLine finds the shift, ranks who can cover it by your rules, and offers it to three caregivers at a time. The first yes gets the shift. If nobody takes it in time, CareLine calls your on-call coordinator.

Shown on demo data. To work with your clients' data, we sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with you.

EVV check before billing

Upload your visit export. CareLine checks each visit against the state's EVV rules and lists what is wrong and how to fix it, before the claim goes out. The demo uses New York and North Carolina rules.

How each process runs

When an applicant calls

You decide

  • Minimum requirements: certificate, area, schedule, languages, experience.
  • Interview times, taken from your calendar.
  • What applicants who don't qualify hear, and who goes on the waitlist.
  • Office hours for transfers to a person.

Names, phone numbers and times are read back to the caller before anything is saved.

When a caregiver calls off

You decide

  • Who can cover whom: skills, languages, weekly hour limits.
  • How many caregivers get each offer (3 by default) and how long each wave waits (15 minutes).
  • When your on-call coordinator gets called (2 hours before the shift by default).
  • The on-call phone chain.

A shift goes to one person only. A caregiver who says yes after it is taken hears that it is taken.

Before you bill

You decide

  • Which state's rules apply. The demo has New York and North Carolina.
  • What to correct and when to bill. CareLine does not change records in your EVV system.

Visits are flagged by fixed rules, not by AI judgment, and every flag comes with the reason and the fix.

Why it matters. North Carolina denies personal care claims without EVV since June 1, 2021, and home health claims in managed care plans since October 1, 2025. New York does not deny claims automatically; the risk there is an OMIG audit and recoupment of payments already made.

Your data and the rules

An AI answers, and says so
Every call opens with "I'm an AI assistant, and this call is recorded." Callers who ask for a person are transferred to your coordinator during office hours; after hours, CareLine takes a message.
Consent on record
Consent to the recording, to talking with an AI and to text messages is logged for each phone number. A STOP reply turns texts off.
Demo data until a BAA is signed
Call-offs, calls from families and the EVV check run on demo data. Before CareLine works with your clients' information, we sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with you.
Texting needs carrier registration
Text offers and reminders start after your agency's number is registered with US carriers (10DLC), which usually takes 5 to 15 business days. Until then, CareLine uses email.
Your rules, your decisions
Screening requirements, interview times, offer waves and on-call timing are your settings. Hiring decisions, billing and EVV corrections stay with your staff.
Lawful questions only
Applicants are asked whether they are authorized to work in the US. They are never asked about citizenship or national origin.
