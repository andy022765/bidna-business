# CareLine
**An AI phone line for New York home care agencies.**

## Your job ads get answered tonight.

Applicants call after your office closes. Aides call off at 5 AM. Visits get flagged after the aide is already paid.
CareLine picks up first: it answers applicants 24/7 in English, Spanish and Russian, screens them by your requirements and books interviews on your calendar. It tells every caller it's an AI, and you see every call in a 7 AM summary.

**Why the phone never stops**
- Home care turnover ran about 75% in 2024 (industry benchmark report).¹ Hiring is a year-round job, not a campaign.
- About 760,500 openings for home health and personal care aides are projected each year, on average, over the decade (BLS).² Every agency is competing for the same applicants.
- States are hardening EVV: North Carolina has rejected home health claims without EVV data since Oct 1, 2025; Missouri started re-processing mismatched claims on May 27, 2026 and recovering payments already made.³

## What CareLine does

## How you start (hiring line)
1. **Fill in our requirement sheet** — about 15 minutes: who you hire, where, schedules, interview slots, how to say no.
2. **Share your interview calendar** — one step, about 2 minutes (Google Calendar).
3. **Put the CareLine number in your job ads.**
You don't switch your scheduling system. Confirmations go by email; texts start once your SMS brand is registered (5–15 business days).

## What stays in your hands
- **Your rules decide.** The line only applies your sheet. Shift decisions come from your rules, and a person confirms.
- **You see everything.** Every call, transcript and decision is in the dashboard.
- **Off switch.** One command sends the line back to your phone. If our system doesn't respond, calls go to your phone. <!-- claim only after the acceptance test call -->
- **No cold calls.** CareLine answers calls. It texts only people who agreed to texts, and the only calls it places are wake-up calls to your own on-call staff, if you set them up.

## What's different
1. **Start without patient data.** The hiring line needs no BAA and no integration.
2. **Three languages from day one:** English, Spanish, Russian.
3. **Rules, not guesses.** Replacements and EVV checks run on rules, not on the AI's judgment; the AI handles the conversation.

## Straight talk
- It's an AI, and it says so on every call.
- We're new. No New York agency uses CareLine yet — you'd be among the first.
- No patient data goes in until a BAA is signed.
- We don't bill Medicaid and never charge a percentage of your revenue or collections.
