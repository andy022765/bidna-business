## 2. The problem — three calls your office can't take

**Section headline:** Three calls your office can't take

**Saturday, 8:00 PM.** An HHA calls about your job ad. The office is closed and the voicemail box is full. On Monday your recruiter calls back — and she's already started somewhere else.

**Tuesday, 4:55 AM.** An aide texts that she can't make her 8 AM shift. Somebody — usually you — opens the spreadsheet and starts texting down the list.

**The 30th of the month.** Billing finds visits with a missing clock-out or the wrong location. The aide was paid weeks ago. Now someone has to fix it before the claim goes out — if it still can be fixed.

## 4. What CareLine does

**Section headline:** One line, set up around your rules

**Hiring line — available for a pilot**
Answers applicants 24/7 in English, Spanish and Russian. Asks your screening questions: certificate (HHA, PCA, CNA), area and transport, days and hours, languages, experience, and work authorization in allowed wording only. Politely turns away people who don't meet your requirements, the way you tell it to. Books the rest on your interview calendar, reads names, numbers and times back to confirm, sends a confirmation and reminders. No patient data involved.

**Call-offs and replacements — works on demo data today; for your clients' data we sign a BAA**
Takes "I can't come" by phone or text. Your rules rank the replacements — skills, the client's language, distance, hours, overtime limits, consent to texts. Offers the shift by text to a few people at a time, gives it to the first YES and tells the others it's taken. If the shift is still open close to start time, it calls your on-call person, then the next one in your chain.

**Family intake — works on demo data today; for your clients' data we sign a BAA**
Takes calls about new clients: area, hours, payment type, urgency. Books the in-home assessment.

**EVV check before you bill — works on demo data today; for your clients' data we sign a BAA**
Checks your visit export against authorizations and schedule by state rules — the six required EVV data points, clock-in and clock-out, location, duration, overlapping visits, authorization dates and codes — and gives your biller a fix list before you submit. Rules find the problems; the AI only explains them in plain words.

**Dashboard and 7 AM summary — included**
Applicants, bookings, call-offs, EVV exceptions and every call with its transcript.

**Disclosure and consent log — included**
AI disclosure and recording notice on every call, a log of consents to recording and texts, a log of every action.

> *Annotation.* Status labels sit next to each module, not in the fine print. The BAA sentence is the exact wording required for modules that would touch patient data.

## 5. How you start

**Section headline:** Live on your job ads without changing your system

1. **Fill in the requirement sheet.** About 15 minutes: who you hire, where, which schedules, when you interview, how to say no.
2. **Share your interview calendar.** One step, about 2 minutes. (The pilot works with Google Calendar.)
3. **Put the CareLine number in your job ads.** That's it.

Confirmations go out by email from day one. Text messages start once your agency's SMS brand is registered with the carriers, which takes 5 to 15 business days.

For call-offs and EVV (after the BAA): a daily export of your schedule and visits. Integrations come later.

## 6. What stays in your hands

**Section headline:** You set the rules. A person makes the calls that matter.

- **Your sheet, your words.** CareLine answers from your requirement sheet. If it doesn't know, it takes a message instead of guessing.
- **Your rules pick replacements.** The AI handles the conversation; shift decisions come from rules you set, and a person confirms.
- **You see every call.** Transcripts, decisions and consents are in the dashboard.
- **People can reach a person.** During office hours, callers who ask for a human are transferred to your coordinator. After hours, CareLine takes a message and schedules a callback.
- **Off switch.** One command sends the line back to your phone. If our system doesn't respond, calls go to your phone. <!-- publish only after the acceptance test call -->

## 7. What CareLine won't do

- **It won't cold-call anyone.** It answers calls. Texts go only to people who agreed to them, and the only calls it places are wake-up calls to your own on-call staff, if you set them up.
- **It won't make hiring decisions.** It screens by your sheet and books interviews. You hire.
- **It won't touch patient data before a BAA is signed.**
- **It won't bill Medicaid,** and we never charge a percentage of your revenue or collections.
- **It won't replace your coordinator.** It takes the first touch and the 5 AM texts so your people start the day with a list, not a fire.

> *Annotation.* This block answers the three fears from PORTRET.md (patient data, switching systems, "the robot will mess up") and keeps us clear of "replaces staff" claims (c-0610).

Yes. It says so at the start of every call, along with a notice that the call is recorded.

No. It takes first contact and the early-morning texts. Your people make the decisions and handle what it can't.

**What about HIPAA?**
The hiring line doesn't handle patient data. Call-offs, family intake and the EVV check work on demo data today; before any of your clients' data goes in, we sign a BAA, and your attorney can review it first.

**Do I have to change my scheduling or EVV system?**
No. The hiring line doesn't touch it. Call-offs and the EVV check start from a daily export; integrations come later.

**Which languages does it speak?**
English, Spanish and Russian.

**What if it gets something wrong?**
It answers only from your sheet and takes a message when it doesn't know. It reads names, numbers and times back to confirm. Shift decisions come from your rules and a person confirms. You can read every transcript.
