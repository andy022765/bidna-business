FAQs

How does AI customer service pricing work at Hatch?

A platform fee plus usage. Hatch AI customer service pricing runs in three annual tiers paid monthly per location (Standard, Pro, and Enterprise) and usage covers contacts, AI conversations, and messaging. Hatch quotes on your locations and volume. You can request a quote to see how the Hatch AI CSR platform turns customer communication into a revenue engine.

What is included in each Hatch pricing tier?

Every Hatch pricing tier includes multichannel AI CSRs across voice, text, and email, journey campaign automation, custom campaigns and targeting, the multi-inbox command center, AI setup and support, and ROI reporting. Pro adds volume, custom reporting, and marketing blasts; Enterprise adds multi-location analytics and oversight.

Does AI call answering cost more across multiple locations?

Plans are priced per location and paid monthly on an annual agreement, so AI call answering cost scales with the number of locations you run. Enterprise is the tier built for multiple locations, divisions, or service areas, and portfolio or franchise agreements get scoped directly, because the structure depends on brands, locations, and volume.

Which usage drives the cost of AI receptionist pricing?

Contacts, AI conversations, and messaging volume. Every Hatch plan is a platform fee plus usage, billed annually and paid monthly per location, with a set amount of each included, and Pro includes more contacts, AI conversations, and messaging than Standard. AI receptionist pricing therefore scales with how much conversation your business actually needs, not how many people you add.

When does billing begin under Hatch pricing, and for how long?

Every Hatch plan is an annual agreement, billed monthly per location. Contract length, the exact billing start date, and renewal or cancellation terms are set deal-by-deal in your agreement, since they depend on your locations, tiers, and start date. Your Hatch rep will confirm these specifics before you sign, so nothing is left ambiguous at go-live.

Can AI customer service pricing start smaller and scale up later?

Yes. Standard, Pro, and Enterprise are built to move between as volume and locations grow, each tier adding contact and conversation capacity on top of the one below. Hatch AI customer service pricing doesn't include a published pilot or trial programme, so scope a starting tier with our sales team.
