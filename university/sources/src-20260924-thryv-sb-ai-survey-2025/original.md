# Thryv AI Adoption Survey Statistics (WebFetch extraction)

## Key Statistics
- AI adoption jumped from 39% (2024) to 55% (2025)—a 41% increase
- Companies with 10-100 employees saw usage rise from 47% to 68%
- 80% of SMB AI users believe AI is essential for reaching new customers
- 78% say AI is necessary to meet consumer expectations for speed and personalization
- 63% use AI daily
- 62% use AI for data analysis
- 55% use AI for content generation
- 46% use AI for customer engagement tools like chatbots
- 58% report saving over 20 hours monthly
- 66% save between $500-$2,000 monthly
- 67% agree AI reduces pressure on themselves and staff
- 46% say AI will make them less reliant on employees
- 14% believe AI could replace an employee
- 42% are open to replacement under right conditions
- 41% believe AI helps navigate economic uncertainty
- Data security concerns decreased 40% year over year

## Survey Methodology
Conducted May 4-14, 2025 with 540 respondents. Participants were business decision-makers aged 21+, in business 1+ years, with 1-100 employees and revenue of $100k-$9.9 million.
