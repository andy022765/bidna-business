Frequently asked questions

Why does your answering service cost more than the competition?

Our AI answering service costs a little bit more because it does a lot more.

Unlike others offering AI that stops at answering basic questions (assuming they even get them right, which most don’t), you can trust us with your business’ reputation.

First, we have humans in the loop. With our AI Receptionist, your calls are always handled by our AI first, but if something complex comes up, the call can be transferred to a live North America-based receptionist — any time, day or night. This built-in human backup ensures your callers get the help they need while you never get interrupted. It’s the perfect blend of efficiency and reliability.

Second, our system is designed specifically to qualify your leads based on our industry-leading experience answering over 20 million calls for businesses in the past 10 years. So yes, it’s a bit pricier than the competition — because we’re committed to making every interaction exceptional.

Do spam calls count towards my quota?

Every call answered by your AI Receptionist counts toward your call quota by default. Smith.ai offers AI Receptionist customers an allowance to remove up to 10% of their calls from their bill each billing cycle to account for selected spam calls. Calls from 20 million+ known spammers are automatically filtered out by our system before your AI Receptionist answers, and these calls do not count toward your quota. You can learn more about this here.

How do you determine if calls are spam?

With technology! Our system automatically blocks over 20 million spam and robocalls from ever getting through. If a new spam number does get through, you can mark it as spam to automatically block the caller and add it to your spam list, so it can’t get through ever again.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. AI Receptionist is a month-to-month service. This means there aren’t any long-term contracts. You pay for the AI Receptionist service each month. If you ever cancel, we need 30 days’ notice, and then you’re done. And we will miss you.

Can I change my plan?

Yes, at any time. We make it easy. If you would like to change your plan, just email support@smith.ai. We’ll start billing for your new plan the next month.

Why do you bill by the call?

Because unlike per-minute services, our success is aligned with your success. Billing per call means there’s no mystery accounting involved. You’re trusting us to represent your business. We’re optimized for the exact right length of calls, not more or less. Per-minute services work opposite to your needs; they want to maximize your call length while you want to minimize your call time.

How is this worth it?

Do you want your business to succeed? Well, good. We do, too. A successful business can’t afford to miss a single lead or sales opportunity. (By the time you call them back, they’ve likely found someone else… or if they’re really bad, they’ll leave a negative review because you didn’t answer.) Our AI Receptionist isn’t just another AI; it’s the only one on the market designed specifically for lead qualification and on-demand escalation to Smith.ai’s 24/7 Live Agent Network. If your callers need more than our AI can handle, AI Receptionist can immediately transfer them to an experienced live agent at your discretion. No missed calls, no frustrated customers — just flawless support. And here’s the best part: it’s a fraction of the cost of an in-house receptionist, yet it works 24/7 and never takes a lunch break. So, you’re not just paying for an answering service; you’re investing in a solution that’s always on, always reliable, and always working for you.

Do you offer discounted plans?

We offer volume discounts and can create a custom plan to suit your high-volume needs. Please book a free consultation with our team.
