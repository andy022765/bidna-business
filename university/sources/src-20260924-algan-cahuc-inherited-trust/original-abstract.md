Algan, Yann, and Pierre Cahuc. 2010. "Inherited Trust and Growth." American Economic Review 100 (5): 2060-92.
Abstract (key finding, per AEA page): inherited trust of descendants of US immigrants is significantly influenced by the country of origin and the timing of arrival of their forebears; the authors use this to estimate a causal effect of inherited trust on growth over the twentieth century.
