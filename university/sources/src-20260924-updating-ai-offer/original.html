    Frequently asked questions
    How quickly will I see results?

    Most businesses start seeing referrals from AI like ChatGPT within 30 days. If you don't start getting leads/potential customers within 30 days, we offer a 30 day money back guarantee.

    What AI platforms will I receive customers from?

    We currently support 16+ of the most popular AI platforms including ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity and more. Our team is always working on supporting the newest platforms and models.

    How do I track results?

    You'll be able to track AI referred visitors directly on your preferred website's analytics platform like Google Analytics, Jobber, ServiceTitan, etc.

    What if I don't receive referrals?

    Getting referred by AI platforms isn't guaranteed. Certain special categories like politics, religion, pharmaceuticals, adult or self-harm content may be banned or suppressed by AI models. If you do not start seeing referrals within 30 days, email our team for a full refund.
    What businesses will this work for? Will it work for me?

    Updating.ai supports 57+ major industries/niches as well as most ultra niche businesses.

    The vast majority of businesses are accepted by AI models, however there are some special categories that are typically suppressed or disallowed like politics, religion, pharmaceuticals, adult or self-harm content. The exact list is fluid and changes over time as the Trust and Safety policies of different models refine over time.

    If you're unsure, you're welcome to email us to ask or sign up to try us out. We'll offer a full refund if we're unable to help drive recommendations from AI platforms within 30 days.
    What's your cancellation policy?

    We offer hassle-free cancellations. To cancel, login to your account and click on 'Cancel'. You'll receive a confirmation email shortly after.
    What's your refund policy?

    We offer full refunds if we're unable to help drive AI recommendations for your business within 30 days.

    To request a refund, just send us an email. Refunds are issued within 2 business days.
