Frequently asked questions
What’s included in the 30-day free trial?
Everything in the plan, free for 30 days — your cloud-hosted AI Knowledge Base, Auto Knowledge Builder, connectivity, analytics, and support. No charge upfront, and you can cancel anytime.
Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?
Yes, anytime. Annual works out to 20% less per month. Moving from monthly to annual starts the new cycle right away with a prorated adjustment; switching back takes effect at your next billing cycle.
What’s your cancellation policy?
Cancel anytime and keep access through the end of your billing period. You won’t be charged during the first 30 days.
What is MCP access?
MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the open standard AI assistants use to read outside knowledge. Courtyard exposes your AI Knowledge Base over MCP, so assistants like ChatGPT and Claude can read your business directly.
Is there a setup fee?
No. The price is all-in, and you can be set up in minutes with the Auto Knowledge Builder.
