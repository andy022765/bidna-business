FAQs

ElevenAgents plans are billed by call minutes, not by the shared credit pool. Included call minutes and concurrent calls per plan: Free 15 minutes / 4 concurrent calls; Starter 75 minutes / 6; Creator 275 minutes / 10; Pro 1,238 minutes / 20; Scale 3,738 minutes / 30; Business 12,375 minutes / 40. Additional call minutes cost $0.08 per minute (burst pricing $0.16 per minute, double the standard rate, when you exceed your concurrency limit), and text messages cost $0.003 each. LLM usage is billed separately on top, based on the model you choose. ElevenLabs does not charge extra for telephony, but if you connect an external provider such as Twilio or a SIP trunk, that provider bills you directly for its own charges.

There is no limit to the number of agents you can create on any plan. You are only limited by the number of concurrent calls available on your plan along with your monthly credit limit.

With ElevenLabs agents, you may select from supported third-party models or you may connect a custom model with a server integration. You may also select optional LLM-enabled features to use with agents. Applicable fees for model usage are shown in the platform's user interface and will be deducted from your ElevenLabs credits.

No. Telephony is included on every plan, and phone calls are billed at the same call-minute rate as any other channel, so ElevenLabs adds no telephony fee. You connect your own provider, such as Twilio or a SIP trunk, and that provider bills you directly for the carrier side of the call at whatever rate you have with them.

Burst pricing allows your ElevenLabs agents to temporarily exceed your workspace's subscription concurrency limit during high-demand periods. When enabled, your agents can handle up to 3 times your normal concurrency limit, with excess calls charged at double the standard rate.

If you upgrade to a higher plan, any unused credits from your previous plan will roll over into the next billing cycle. If you downgrade or cancel your subscription, your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle. After that, your account will be downgraded to the Free plan and any unused paid credits will expire.

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle, after which it will not renew and your account will be downgraded to the Free plan.

You can view your remaining credits by logging into the platform and navigating to your subscription page from your profile menu.

We accept Credit Card, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Subscriptions are billed on a monthly or yearly basis starting from the day you subscribe.
