# Trucking in the U.S.A.: Where the Accent Is Russian (TIME, 2010-09-04, Pat Dawson)

- No precise count exists; Russian-speaking drivers are described as "a distinct and growing presence" in cross-country freight trucking.
- Context: after the 2008 crisis the number of trucking companies in Russia fell from 10,000+ to ~8,000; ASMAP spokeswoman: this caused "something of an exodus among Russian truckers".
- Example: Mikhail Trucking (Spokane, WA), founded 2006 by Russian brothers, 7 trucks, 48 states + western Canada. Owner Nicolay Karpov: trucking is "the easiest way to make some good money" for immigrants without English fluency or credentials.
- Median trucker wage 2009: $37,730.
- Clustering explained by the traditional immigrant pattern of filling a niche.
