FAQs

Find answers to your questions about pricing and subscription management.

Do you have monthly and annual subscriptions?

Yes, you can purchase OtterlyAI subscriptions monthly or in an annual subscription.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, we provide a free trial for new users. This allows you to explore our platform and its features without any commitment. Sign up today to start your trial.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. Simply navigate to the subscription section and follow the cancellation process. If you need assistance, our support team is here to help. All subscriptions are on a monthly basis. You can therefore also cancel your monthly subscription anytime.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all standard credit cards. All transactions are secure and encrypted for your safety. For additional payment options, please contact our support team.

Are there any hidden fees?

No, we believe in transparency. All fees are clearly outlined in our pricing plans, and there are no hidden charges. You don't need any additional OpenAI subscriptions You can review all costs before committing to a subscription.

How many prompts do I really need?

It generally depends on the number of markets, languages and products you want to track and optimize on AI Search. Make sure to check out our prompt calculator here to get an estimate on how many prompts your business needs to monitor.
