A Frontdesk plan that answers phone calls costs $99 per month, or $79 per month billed annually, and includes 200 voice minutes a month alongside chat, SMS, CRM, and automations.

What the rest of the market charges

Published AI receptionist prices start at around $14 per month and rise with the minutes included. ai-receptionist.com advertises plans from $14 per month and Allo from $18 per month. AgentZap runs from $25 to $899 per month, NextPhone from $199 to $299 per month, and Imagicle from $25 to $3,000 per month.

Frontdesk sits at $99 per month for the plan that answers calls, and that figure also covers web chat, 2-way SMS, an AI-native CRM, ticketing, and sequence automations rather than voice alone.

Competitor prices are each vendor's own published rate, last checked August 2026. Compare what each tier includes rather than the headline price alone, because entry tiers differ widely in the minutes they allow.
