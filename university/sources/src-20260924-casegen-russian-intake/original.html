Support Your Russian-Speaking Clients with Confidence

In today’s legal landscape, clear communication is everything. If your firm handles personal injury, immigration, family, or criminal law, there’s a growing chance your clients speak Russian as their primary language. CaseGen.ai offers an advanced Russian answering service for law firms, built to eliminate missed calls, language confusion, and lost opportunities—24/7, 365 days a year.

Why a Russian-Speaking Answering Service Is Essential

The legal intake call is often the first impression a client has of your law firm. If that client speaks Russian and no one on your team does, the opportunity may be lost—fast. Worse, the caller may feel dismissed or unwelcome.

That’s where CaseGen.ai comes in. With our AI-powered Russian virtual receptionist, you can offer fluid, natural Russian communication without hiring a full-time staff member or relying on unpredictable call centers.

Key Benefits of Our Russian Answering Service

24/7 Availability: Never miss a call from a Russian-speaking lead, no matter the hour.

Legal Intake Trained:  Our AI agents are trained by legal marketing experts to gather only the most relevant case details.

Human-Like Natural Language: The Russian sounds fluent and comforting, not robotic, thanks to advanced voice modeling.

Transcript Translation: All Russian transcripts are automatically translated into English and sent to your CRM, inbox, or case management system.

High-Value Case Screening: We identify key intake signals (accident type, injury, coverage, court date) on the very first call.

How CaseGen.ai Handles Russian-Speaking Intakes

Our AI detects the caller’s language—or gives them the option to continue in Russian. Once selected, the agent follows a legal intake script tailored for Russian-speaking clients.

Typical Russian Intake Questions Include:

❝Kogda proiskhodiya avariya?❞
(“When did the accident happen?”)

❝Gde eto sluchilos’?❞
(“Where did it happen?”)

❝Vy obrashchalis’ k vrachu posle proisshestviya?❞
(“Did you see a doctor after the incident?”)

❝Kto byl vinovnik?❞
(“Who was at fault?”)

❝Vy uzhe razgovarivali s drugim advokatom?❞
(“Have you spoken with another attorney?”)

All answers are saved, translated, and sent to your team for immediate action.

FAQs About Russian Bilingual Legal Answering Services

What is a Russian bilingual legal intake service?

It’s a 24/7 phone intake system that allows Russian-speaking clients to explain their legal issues in their native language. CaseGen uses AI to manage these calls accurately and professionally.

Can the AI speak both English and Russian fluently?

Yes. The AI agent can detect or switch between English and Russian seamlessly. It understands legal vocabulary and collects relevant case data in both languages.

What types of cases can the Russian AI agent handle?

Our Russian-speaking agent handles personal injury, immigration, family, workers’ comp, and criminal law matters. Each script is customized to your firm’s specialties.

Is this service more affordable than hiring staff?

Absolutely. Law firms save thousands annually by replacing live answering services with our scalable, usage-based AI model.

Can I review Russian transcripts in English?

Yes. Every call is recorded, transcribed in Russian, and translated into English before being delivered to your team or CRM.

What areas does CaseGen currently support?

We currently support English, Spanish, and Russian—with more languages coming soon.
